WHERE IT STARTED

Before the job titles.

My journey in technology started while I was still completing my Computer Science degree. I worked as a computer technician while studying, supporting users and learning how business technology worked outside the classroom.

The work grew into laying UTP network cabling, installing company servers, supporting day-to-day operations, and running nighttime data-processing workloads on Clipper-based systems—including indexing and preparing daily sales information from departments for management reporting.

I studied during the day and worked with real systems after hours. That hands-on beginning became the foundation for everything that followed: teaching Computer Science, web development, entrepreneurship, enterprise software, FinTech, technical leadership, and now AI-assisted engineering.

SOFTWARE ENGINEERING IN THE AI ERA

AI can generate code.
Engineers still own the outcome.

My workflow has increasingly shifted from manually producing every implementation toward directing AI-assisted development: defining intent, architecture and constraints; reviewing generated solutions; enforcing engineering standards; validating behavior through tests; and making sure what reaches production remains understandable and maintainable.

I see this as an evolution of the engineer's role—from primarily producing code to increasingly designing, directing, validating and owning software systems.
